## The plot-session launcher

Use the `plot-session` skill for the launcher command, its options, native Git approval policy, delivery exclusion and Codex trust entry. `README.md` and `docs/session-walkthrough.md` explain session delivery and recovery.

What a kickoff owes a session and what an issue owes it is the contract in `docs/contracts.md`, and `task kickoff:check -- <file>` decides a kickoff before its launch.
